# Light the Torch — upbeat start screen song

Status: archived in the repo only, not used by the game, pending Anthony's final approval.

Game: Ashen Depths. Feel: upbeat, heroic, sword-and-sorcery adventure. ~60 s loop, about 140 BPM.

[Verse]
Rusty sword and a cloak of grey,
Stairs go down where the embers play,
Goblins grin and the skeletons sway,
But we're not turning back today!

[Chorus]
Light the torch, take the stairs,
Into the Depths if you dare!
Through the ash and the fire we go,
Ashen Depths, here we go!

[Tag]
Morgana, Vesper, Isolde too,
Sir Hollow, Ashkar, we're coming for you!
Tap to start, the night is young,
The bravest songs are still unsung!

[Chorus, repeat to loop]
